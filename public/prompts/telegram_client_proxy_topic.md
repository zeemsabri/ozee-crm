Connecting Client DMs to Internal TopicsWith the new telegram_topics table, your Laravel backend acts as a two-way mirror.Single Bot vs. Two BotsOption A: Single Bot (The "Omni-Bot") - Easiest SetupYou just use @OZee_Web_Bot for everything.Clients search for @OZee_Web_Bot and send it a Direct Message.Your team adds @OZee_Web_Bot to the internal project groups.Pros: Only one webhook to manage, one token to maintain.Cons: Your internal bot has the exact same name/profile picture as your client-facing support bot.The Two-Way Proxy Logic (How Laravel connects them)The logic in your Laravel Webhook Controller is: You just need to check where the message came from, and where it needs to go.Direction 1: Client -> Internal Team TopicWhen the webhook receives a Direct Message (DM):Identify Sender: Laravel sees it's a DM and looks up the telegram_id in the telegram_accounts table to find the associated Client.Check Context: Laravel checks the client's active_telegram_project_id. (Check if we require new field)Find the Proxy Topic: Laravel queries the telegram_topics table for project_id = X AND type = 'client_proxy'.Relay the Message: Laravel takes the client's text and tells the bot to post it into that specific thread_id in your team's internal group.UI Bonus: Laravel prefixes the message with 💬 **[Client Name]:** so your team knows who said it!Direction 2: Internal Team -> Client DMWhen the webhook receives a message from inside a Group Thread:Identify the Topic: Laravel looks at the incoming message_thread_id and checks the telegram_topics table.Check the Topic Type:If type == 'internal': Laravel just saves the message to the database for your CRM UI. It does NOT send anything to the client.If type == 'client_proxy': Laravel knows this thread is the bridge. It finds the project, finds the primary client for that project, grabs the client's telegram_id from their linked telegram_account, and tells the bot to send them a Direct Message!Relay the Message: The client's phone buzzes with a DM from the bot.Team Transparency: The bot formats the message to include the internal team member's name (e.g., "💬 Sarah: [message]") so the client knows exactly who they are talking to within the agency, while keeping the contractor's direct contact details completely private.
