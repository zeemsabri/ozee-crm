### Purpose / audience — IMPORTANT conflict with the guide
The guide's inventory table (§1.3) lists Home A/B as **Internal · "My work"**. The mock content is **not** an internal OZee staff home. It is a **client-tenant home**: header workspace name "Harper & Vale" (a law firm), signed-in user `Avatar text="Ellen Harper"`, greeting "Good morning, Ellen", and the queue contains *her firm's* leads/bookings plus an item titled **"OZee needs your approval"** ("The new services page layout is ready for you to look over before we build it."). A **two-level admin**:
1. **Platform level** ("OZee staff only" scope, `ws = ozee`): OZee runs a **multi-tenant SaaS** — other agencies ("workspaces": Meridian Digital, Southbank Studio, Kite & Co, Brightpath Marketing) run the same product under **plans** (Studio / Agency / Network), optionally **white-labelled**, with an **OZee support access** switch, and a separate **Platform team** identity (Platform owner / Onboarding / Support engineer / Billing admin).
2. **Workspace (agency) level** — for OZee's own agency and for any tenant when "entered": **Clients → Businesses → Projects** (with tiers Care/Growth/Partner per *project*), **Finance** (proposals, invoices, bills, service catalogue, Xero, multi-currency), **Domains & DNS** (providers, records, guardrails), **People & Teams**, **Roles & permissions** (action matrix, levels 0–4, guardrails), **Access requests**, **Ghost sessions**, **Audit log**.

The whole console models a **three-ceiling permission system**: *Plan entitlement* (set by OZee) → *Client tier* (set by the agency, per project) → *Role & level* (set by a manager, never above their own). This is the single most important spec in the export for the backend (see 5.5.26).

**Conflict with the existing app:** the existing CRM has no multi-tenancy, no plans, no businesses layer, no tiers, no role levels/ceilings, no ghost login, no domains module, no proposals→bills→Xero chain with multi-currency expendables. Almost all of 5.5 is **new scope**. Recommend the planner split it: (A) agency-workspace admin needed for OZee itself (Clients/Businesses/Projects/Finance/Domains/People/Roles/Audit), (B) platform/multi-tenant layer (Workspaces/Plans/Platform team/white-label/support access) as a later phase.

### 5.5.26 Business rules the mock encodes (backend spec)
1. **Tier derivation**: business tier = strictest (lowest rank) tier of its projects; client tier = strictest across all businesses it owns/co-owns; spread text "Partner on 2 · Growth on 1"; "No projects yet" → Care.
2. **Permission check** for user U doing action A on client C: `plan.modules ∋ A.module` (workspace ceiling; module may also be switched off per workspace) **and** `A ∉ TIER_BLOCKS[C.tier]` **and** `A ∈ role(U).grants` (base MATRIX ± per-role overrides). Guarded actions additionally require: `Approval` → creates an **access request** for an approver whose level ≥ `need` and who isn't tier-blocked; `Re-auth` → password/2FA re-prompt; `Logged` → audit entry; `Reason` → free-text reason stored (ghost); `Ceiling` → grantor must hold ≥ what is granted.
3. **Delegation ceiling**: a user can only grant/invite/create roles at or below their own level; can only toggle matrix cells for roles ≤ own rank; L≥2 to give access, L≥3 for DNS edit, business move/archive, team client assignment, team modules; L4 for domain transfer, plan/billing, client delete.
4. **Reach**: staff inherit every client their teams serve; contractors get named clients only (direct grants), a mandatory end date (≤90 days on invite, extension = request), 2FA required, counted as a seat while live.
5. **Contact reach** per project decides what a client contact sees; only named contacts see the project; "Billing" contacts are the invoice recipients; "Approve deliverables" contacts are the approver options.
6. **Plan scaffolding**: creating a project from service packs generates milestones→deliverables→tasks from PACK_PLAN; "Duplicate a project" copies the plan shifting dates; "Empty" needs a team.
7. **Budget model**: budget is never typed — service budget = Σ expendables (converted to AUD at the daily rate); project budget = Σ service budgets; proposals can only be **approved** if `amount ≤ service budget − committed`; approved proposals add their contractor to the project team; bills draw against a proposal (or are direct supplier costs).
8. **Bill state machine**: `Needs coding` (missing Xero account or tax) → `Awaiting approval` (chain by AUD size: >$1k +dept head, >$5k +owner; each approver stamps a time; the next approver must have rank ≥ step) → `Ready to sync` → `Synced to Xero` (XB- id) → `Paid` (bank ref; without ref shows "Not reconciled") | `Rejected`. GST = amount/11 for "GST on expenses (10%)".
9. **Invoice model**: invoices are raised only from a service's **payment terms** (percent split of the client price); raising marks those terms `Invoiced` and they cannot be picked again; Draft → "Sync to Xero & send" (XI- id) → Awaiting payment/Overdue → payments attached from an unreconciled bank feed (part payments leave a balance) → Paid. Repricing a service only moves `Not raised` terms; changing terms cannot drop below the number of already-raised rows.
10. **Domains**: visible to a client only when assigned to one of their businesses; team reach on a domain is separate from project access; MX/NS/SPF/DMARC records are guarded (approval by L3 before writing to the provider; old value kept 90 days); providers with API sync daily at 06:00 AWST, manual ones are flagged amber; expiring ≤60 days flagged.
11. **Ghost session**: read-only by default; elevation = one task, 15 minutes, reason required, client owner emailed; billing and domain transfer never available; every action logged (Opened/Read/Elevated/Write).
