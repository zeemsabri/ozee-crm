Technical Specification: Mapping Existing Xero Repeating and Standalone Invoices1. The ChallengeTo ensure your CRM calculates accurate project-level margins, it needs a complete financial history of every project. Currently, there are two primary disconnects between Xero and your CRM:Repeating Templates: Xero has active, auto-generating repeating templates that produce invoices natively with no existing reference or link to CRM projects.Historical Standalone Invoices & Bills: Past standalone client invoices (sales) and contractor bills (expenses) created in Xero prior to connecting the CRM are unlinked, preventing a true retrofitted profit-and-loss calculation.This specification outlines a unified mapping utility that allows admins to securely link both legacy repeating templates and standalone historic transactions to their respective CRM Projects, Clients, and Contractors.2. Step 1: Discovery & SearchTo fetch legacy structures without hitting API limits or bloating local storage, provide two targeted tabs in the CRM Admin panel:Repeating Templates Discovery: Pulls active templates from Xero in real-time.Endpoint: GET /RepeatingInvoicesAction: Discovers and stores repeating invoice definitions temporarily in a xero_repeating_templates staging state with a null project ID.Standalone Invoice & Bill On-Demand Search:Provide a clean input field letting admins search Xero for specific invoices or bills using a dynamic contact name or invoice number filter.API Query Filter: GET /Invoices?where=(Contact.Name.Contains("{query}") OR InvoiceNumber=="{query}") AND Status!="VOIDED"This retrieves only matching metadata on demand, avoiding bulk table downloads.Step 2: The Unified Mapping UIOnce items are discovered, admins can map them directly to CRM resources:For Invoices & Repeating Templates: The admin selects the Xero row and pairs it with a CRM Client and Project using searchable dropdowns.For Bills: The admin pairs the Xero Bill with a CRM Contractor, Project, and optionally links it to a specific project_expendables_id (contract/proposal). If linked to an expendable, the bill amount will dynamically reduce the remaining budget balance of that milestone.Step 3: Historical Database SyncUpon confirming the mapping in the UI, the CRM dispatches a queued job SyncHistoricalXeroItemJob to safely bring over records and their matching transaction states.For Standalone Invoices/Bills:Verify if the xero_invoice_id already exists locally.If Yes: Update its foreign keys (project_id, client_id, contractor_id, project_expendable_id).If No: Write a new record to the local invoices or bills table including all line items, sub-totals, tax amounts, and status.Query Xero for payments associated with that specific invoice: GET /Invoices/{InvoiceID}.For each mapped payment:Write an entry in the local transactions table, mapping is_paid = 1, and capturing the official reconciliation date from Xero.For Repeating Templates:Persist the template in xero_repeating_templates linked to the chosen CRM project.Query Xero for all occurrences generated by that specific template:GET /Invoices?where=RepeatingInvoiceID==GUID("{id}")Map and save all past standard invoice occurrences to your local invoices and transactions tables to backfill historical earnings.3.
